# Movie Discovery & Cinema Formats

## Cinema Formats & Audio Technology
EpicShow partners with premier exhibition chains to offer diverse screening formats:
- **IMAX 3D / IMAX 2D**: Massive curved screens, proprietary geometry, and dual 4K laser projection delivering unparalleled visual clarity and immersion.
- **4DX Motion Seats**: Environmental sensory effects including synchronized motion seats, wind gusts, water mist, fog, strobe lighting, and ambient scents.
- **Dolby Atmos**: Next-generation object-based sound technology placing individual audio elements anywhere in 3D acoustic space, including overhead speakers.
- **Gold Class & Recliner Lounges**: Ultra-plush motorized recliners providing maximum viewing comfort.
- **Standard 2D & RealD 3D**: Crisp digital projection with high frame rate (HFR) clarity for all cinematic releases.

## Languages & Regional Cinema
EpicShow celebrates global and regional Indian cinema with native language selections:
- **Languages Supported**: Hindi, English, Telugu, Tamil, Malayalam, Kannada, Marathi, Punjabi, Bengali, and Gujarati.
- **Subtitled Screenings**: English subtitles (EST) are indicated on showtime chips to assist multilingual moviegoers.

## Censor Certification & Age Restrictions
- **U (Universal)**: Suitable for viewers of all age groups without restriction.
- **UA (Universal Under Parental Guidance)**: Suitable for general viewing; parents are advised to guide children under 12 years.
- **A (Adults Only)**: Restricted strictly to patrons aged 18 years and above. Valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, Driving License, Passport) is mandatory at venue entry.
- **S (Specialized Audience)**: Restricted to specialized professional or scientific audiences.
