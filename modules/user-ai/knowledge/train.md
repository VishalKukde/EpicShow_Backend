# Train & Transport Booking Information

## Railway Route Search & Schedule Discovery
EpicShow integrates railway ticketing across major Indian railway networks:
- **Search Capabilities**: Search trains between origin and destination stations, filter by departure time, travel duration, train types (Vande Bharat, Rajdhani, Shatabdi, Duronto, Superfast, Mail/Express), and seat availability.
- **Real-Time Seat Availability**: Check live vacancy status, current confirmation probabilities, and general quota schedules.

## Travel Classes & Berth Types
EpicShow supports all standard railway accommodation classes:
- **1A (AC First Class)**: Premium lockable 2-berth coupes and 4-berth cabins with attendant calls and bedding.
- **2A (AC Two Tier)**: Air-conditioned coaches with 2 tiers of berths on each side (Lower & Upper, plus Side Lower & Side Upper), curtains for privacy, and reading lights.
- **3A (AC Three Tier)**: Air-conditioned coaches with 3 tiers (Lower, Middle, Upper, plus Side Lower & Side Upper), complete with fresh linen packs.
- **3E (AC 3 Economy)**: Economical air-conditioned coaches offering comfortable travel at reduced fares.
- **CC (AC Chair Car)**: Reclining plush aircraft-style seats ideal for daytime intercity routes like Vande Bharat and Shatabdi Express.
- **SL (Sleeper Class)**: Non-air-conditioned open-window sleeper coaches with classic 3-tier berths.

## PNR Status & Confirmation Terms
- **PNR (Passenger Name Record)**: A unique 10-digit number assigned to every railway booking.
- **CNF (Confirmed)**: Seat and berth allocation is guaranteed.
- **RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation)**: Travelers are permitted to board the train with shared seating, with high likelihood of full berth allotment upon charting.
- **WL (Waitlist)**: Ticket is waitlisted. If the ticket does not move to RAC/CNF by the time the final chart is prepared (typically 4 hours before train departure), the ticket is automatically cancelled and refunded in full.

## Charting & Boarding Rules
- **Chart Preparation**: First charts are prepared 4 hours prior to scheduled train departure from the originating station. Second charts are finalized 30 minutes before departure.
- **Valid Identification**: At least one passenger on the PNR ticket must carry an original government ID (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, Driving License) during the journey.
