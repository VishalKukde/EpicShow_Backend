# Gaming Lounges & Esports Arenas

## Gaming Infrastructure & Rigs
EpicShow allows gamers to book hourly and tournament slots at partnered esports cafes and next-gen gaming arenas:
- **High-End PC Battle Stations**: Powered by NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 / 4080 GPUs, Intel Core i9 / AMD Ryzen 9 processors, ultra-fast NVMe storage, and 240Hz/360Hz low-latency gaming monitors.
- **Console Lounges**: Private comfortable pods equipped with PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X consoles hooked to 65" 4K 120Hz OLED displays with dual-wireless controllers for co-op gaming.
- **Virtual Reality (VR) Zones**: Room-scale VR headsets (Meta Quest Pro, HTC Vive Focus 3, PSVR 2) and full-motion racing cockpit simulators with force-feedback steering wheels and hydraulic pedals.

## Booking Passes & Hourly Packages
- **Hourly Passes**: Reserve 1-hour, 2-hour, or 3-hour continuous sessions on your chosen rig.
- **All-Night LAN Pass**: Special overnight packages (11:00 PM to 7:00 AM) offering unlimited high-speed gaming access.
- **Squad Booking**: Lock 5 adjacent PC battle stations simultaneously for competitive team scrims or ranked matches in titles like Valorant, CS2, Dota 2, and Apex Legends.

## Competitive Tournaments & Community Ladders
- **Weekend Tournaments**: Weekly community brackets with cash prizes and leaderboard rankings.
- **Registration**: Solo and team registrations can be completed directly through EpicShow Gaming, with automatic bracket allocation and check-in QR codes.

## Arena Etiquette & Rules
- Arrive 10 minutes prior to your reserved slot to log into personal Steam, Epic Games, Riot, or Xbox accounts.
- External peripheral mice, keyboards, and headsets may be plugged in, provided no third-party cheat software or unverified USB drives are loaded.
- Gaming lounges are strictly smoke-free and alcohol-free environments.
