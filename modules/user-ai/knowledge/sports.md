# Sports & Live Stadium Events

## Sports Categories & Tournaments
EpicShow provides official ticketing for premier live athletic competitions:
- **Cricket**: International bilateral series, Indian Premier League (IPL) matches, and domestic trophies (Ranji Trophy, Vijay Hazare).
- **Football**: Indian Super League (ISL), I-League fixtures, and international club friendlies.
- **Badminton & Tennis**: National championships, Super Series opens, and ATP/WTA sanctioned fixtures.
- **Racing & Esports**: Karting championships, motorsports rallies, and stadium esports finals.

## Stadium Seating Architecture
- **Stands & Pavilions**: Seating layouts detail North Stand, South Pavilion, East Stand, and West Wing terraces with elevated pitch-line views.
- **VIP Hospitality & Corporate Boxes**: Air-conditioned suites featuring private lounge access and exclusive terrace viewing decks.
- **General Admission**: Open-seating bleacher rows offering energetic fan atmosphere.

## Match Day Rules & Gate Protocols
- **Gate Opening Times**: Stadium entry gates typically open 2 to 3 hours prior to match commencement. Patrons are strongly advised to arrive early to avoid security bottleneck delays.
- **ID Verification**: Every ticket holder must present a valid government-issued photo identification card (Aadhaar, Passport, PAN Card, Voter ID) matching the name on their booking profile.
- **Prohibited Items**: For stadium security, the following items are strictly banned: backpacks, power banks, selfie sticks, coins, water bottles, lighters, flammable materials, fireworks, professional DSLR cameras with detachable zoom lenses, and outside food/liquids.
- **Re-Entry**: Stadiums enforce a strict "No Re-Entry" rule once a ticket's QR code has been scanned at the security turnstiles.
