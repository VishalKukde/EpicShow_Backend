# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Digital E-Tickets & QR Code Entry
### How do I access my tickets after booking?
All confirmed bookings generate an encrypted digital e-ticket immediately upon payment confirmation. You can access your tickets anytime:
1. In the app/website: Go to **Profile > Bookings** where all active and past tickets are organized.
2. In your inbox: An instant email confirmation containing the e-ticket PDF and QR code is sent to your registered email address.
3. Via SMS / WhatsApp: A booking link with your ticket ID and QR code is dispatched to your registered mobile number.

### Do I need to print my ticket?
No physical printout is required. Simply present the digital QR code on your mobile device at cinema turnstiles, stadium security gates, gaming lounge reception, or railway ticket examiners for instant paperless scanning.

## Account Management & Profile Settings
### How do I update my profile details or change my password?
Navigate to **Profile > Settings** to update your name, email address, contact number, or notification preferences. To update your password, use the **Change Password** section under security settings.

### What if I forgot my account password?
Click the **"Forgot Password?"** link on the login modal. Enter your registered email address to receive a secure password reset link valid for 15 minutes.

## Device Compatibility & Experience
### Can I use EpicShow on mobile devices?
Yes, EpicShow is a progressive, fully responsive web application optimized for smartphones, tablets, laptops, and ultra-wide desktops. It includes touch-friendly seating layouts, adaptive drawers, and PWA capabilities.

## Customer Care & Dispute Resolution
### How do I contact EpicShow customer support?
Our customer support team is available 24/7 through multiple channels:
- **Live Support Chat**: Accessible directly from **Profile > Chat** for real-time assistance.
- **Report an Issue**: Submit a ticket under **Profile > Report Issue** for booking disputes, seat concerns, or technical problems.
- **AI Help**: Use the floating **✨ Ask Epic AI** button on the home page for instant policy questions, booking checks, and movie recommendations.
