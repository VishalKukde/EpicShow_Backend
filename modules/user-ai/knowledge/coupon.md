# Coupons, Offers & Loyalty Rewards

## Promo Codes & Instant Discounts
EpicShow regularly provides promotional discount codes to maximize savings on entertainment and travel:
- **Welcome Codes**: New users can apply welcome codes (e.g., `FIRSTSHOW`) for instant flat discounts on their first booking.
- **Category Specials**: Weekend movie discounts (up to 30% off on select screenings) and gaming lounge combo passes.
- **Bank & Wallet Offers**: Partner bank card discounts (e.g., ICICI, HDFC, Axis, SBI) providing Buy-One-Get-One (BOGO) or cashback rewards.

## How to Apply Coupons at Checkout
1. Proceed to the checkout screen after selecting your seats or travel berths.
2. Under the **"Offers & Promo Codes"** section, browse eligible coupons or type your promo code into the input box.
3. Click **"Apply"**. The discounted amount will instantly reflect in your total payable summary before payment.
4. Active collected coupons can also be reviewed at any time under **Profile > Offers**.

## EpicShow Pro Subscription
EpicShow Pro is an exclusive annual loyalty membership designed for frequent entertainment enthusiasts:
- **Zero Convenience Fees**: Enjoy 100% waiver of convenience fees across all movie, sports, and gaming bookings.
- **Priority Customer Support**: Fast-track access to dedicated support managers.
- **Early-Bird Access**: Advance booking windows for blockbuster film releases and high-demand concert/sports fixtures 24 hours before general release.

## Reward Booster Points Program
- **Earn on Every Booking**: All registered patrons earn 5% back as Reward Points on eligible bookings.
- **1:1 Value Redemption**: Points are valued at 1 Point = ₹1 INR and can be redeemed directly at checkout without minimum balance restrictions.
- **No Hidden Expiry**: Earned reward points remain active for 12 months from the date of issue.
