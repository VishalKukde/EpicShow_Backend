## Architectural Philosophy & Engineering Highlights
Vishal Kukde engineered EpicShow with enterprise-grade standards:
- **Decoupled Architecture**: High-speed Next.js App Router (React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS) paired with a robust Express 5 Node.js backend.
- **Real-Time Responsiveness**: Live WebSocket channels for synchronized seat availability, instant session locks, and customer support.
- **Security & Data Integrity**: JWT session synchronization, HTTP-only cookie guards, bcrypt credential encryption, and Razorpay signature verification.
- **Intelligent RAG AI Pipeline**: Context-aware Google Gemini assistant powered by vector similarity search, hybrid retrieval, and real-time database tooling.
