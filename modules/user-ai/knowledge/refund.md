# Cancellation Policies & Refund Timelines

## Category-Wise Cancellation Windows
EpicShow allows customers to cancel confirmed tickets subject to category-specific cutoff windows:

### 1. Movie Tickets
- **Cutoff Window**: Cancellations are permitted up to **2 hours before the scheduled showtime**.
- **Eligibility**: Valid on standard theatrical bookings. Screenings booked with special non-refundable promotional discount codes cannot be cancelled.
- **Deductions**: Applicable cancellation charges (typically ₹20–₹30 per ticket depending on the venue partner) may be deducted; the remaining ticket base price is refunded in full. Convenience fees are non-refundable.

### 2. Sports Matches & Arena Fixtures
- **Cutoff Window**: Cancellations are accepted up to **12 hours prior to the stadium gate opening time**.
- **Post-Cutoff**: Tickets cancelled within 12 hours of the fixture or on match day are strictly non-refundable due to stadium security seat allocations.

### 3. Gaming Lounges & VR Arenas
- **Cutoff Window**: Reservations can be rescheduled or cancelled up to **12 hours before the booked session time**.
- **No-Show Rule**: Missed slots without prior cancellation are non-refundable.

### 4. Train Travel Cancellations
- **IRCTC Rules**: Rail cancellations follow Indian Railways passenger cancellation schedules:
  - More than 48 hours before train departure: Standard flat cancellation fee per passenger.
  - Between 48 hours and 12 hours before departure: 25% deduction of ticket fare.
  - Between 12 hours and 4 hours (or chart preparation): 50% deduction of ticket fare.
  - After chart preparation: No cancellation or refund permitted online (TDR filing required).

## Refund Processing Timelines & Modes
Refunds are initiated automatically the moment a ticket cancellation is approved:
- **EpicShow Wallet**: **Instant credit within 60 seconds**. Funds can be immediately used for any future booking across Movies, Sports, Gaming, or Trains.
- **UPI (GPay / PhonePe / Paytm)**: **1 to 3 business days**, credited directly to the linked bank account.
- **Net Banking**: **2 to 4 working banking days**.
- **Credit & Debit Cards**: **3 to 5 banking working days**, depending on your issuing bank's settlement cycle.

## Real-Time Refund Status Tracking
You can monitor the live progress of any initiated refund by navigating to **Profile > Refunds**. Each refund entry shows:
- Payment ID & Refund Reference Number
- Item Title & Original Booking ID
- Total Refund Amount
- Current Status (`In Progress` or `Completed / Credited`)
- Timestamp of Initiation and Credit
